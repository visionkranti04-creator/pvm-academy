Good to Know

Fees & scholarships

Fee structures vary by grade and are shared in full during your campus visit, along with a breakdown of transport and activity charges. Merit and sibling scholarships are available — ask your admissions counsellor for details.

  • Merit scholarships for top academic performers
  • Sibling discount for multiple children enrolled
  • Need-based fee assistance, case by case
Documents Required

Keep these ready

  • Birth certificate (original + copy)
  • Previous school's transfer certificate & report card
  • Address proof & passport-size photographs
  • Aadhaar card copy (student and parent)
